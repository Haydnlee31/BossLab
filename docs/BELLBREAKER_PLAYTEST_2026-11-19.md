Art direction from the player camera: the giant's size, the large negative space under the overhead weapon and the horizontal sweep silhouette are worth preserving. Make the preparation for Hammer Fall and Quake distinct *before* the active frames: e.g. a stepping shoulder-led fall versus a planted two-foot brace for Quake; the broken-bell transition can have its own held pose. Three attacks currently share the same overhead endpoint path (`BellOffsetAt`); changing the setup and recovery can make the fight easier to read without altering reach or windows. For the sweep, the current anti-flail layer retains only 35% of the clip on the legs and 45% on the pelvis. Author a giant-scale pivot with a fixed lead foot and a trailing heel release, then measure foot translation during contact. Increasing full-body clip weight blindly risks reintroducing the original foot slide.

Outer-ring check (`wave-probe.py` / `wave-probe.json`): for a stationary, grounded player with no i-frames, the crest can damage at **9.8 m** on tick 59 after wave start. Even at the 10 m fade-out radius the brightest possible part of the shader is only at 9.55 m; that bright line therefore **never crosses a player at 9.8 m**, although a faint outer wash can be visible.
