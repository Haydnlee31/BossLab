# Unreal asset provenance

Epic Games UE 5.8.4 installed template mannequin subset (meshes, materials, textures and unarmed animation sequences/blend space). Governed by the [Unreal Engine EULA](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/unreal), whose Examples definition includes Templates. No download, new agreement, purchase or public distribution performed. Files copied without modification at the original /Game/Characters/Mannequins mount paths. See artifacts/unreal/asset-manifest.json for exact paths, sizes and SHA-256 values. Original arena geometry and gameplay source are project-authored. No claim that Epic mannequin art is original BossLab art.

Added installed Rigs/PA_Mannequin.uasset (294 KiB) to resolve skeletal mesh dependency. Hash and original path: artifacts/unreal/physics-asset-manifest.json. Original assets remain unchanged. Attack poses sampled in attack-animation-samples.json; runtime cancels root displacement.

U4 additions are original: eight material instances and parent material authored by scripts/unreal-create-surfaces.py; geometric armor and sanctuary dressing in WardenPresentation.cpp; four mono 22,050 Hz synthesized WAV cues in SourceAudio (no recorded samples or external sound library). The original mannequin skeleton/animation remains Epic template content. Audio assets load in gameplay; auditory quality has not been independently assessed.

Fundamentals revision: the greatsword geometry and runtime keyed full-body sword/roll poses in WardenSword.cpp are original project code. Installed locomotion/idle remains the temporary Epic rig basis. No external weapon, motion-capture or character pack was downloaded. Historical unarmed attacks/assets remain preserved but are not the active sword animation.

Ashbound Knight first pass (2026-11-06): WardenOutfit.cpp authors original polygon armor, mantle/cape and Oathwake geometry (15,448 triangles excluding the underlying Epic body), plus five dynamic surface colors/roughness settings. WardenPrototype.cpp supplies a tiny original ambient cubemap generated from six colors at runtime. No copied source-game meshes/textures, external assets or AI-generated concept art. Existing Epic mannequin/rig/locomotion are retained and remain Epic content. Static OBJ/MTL export contains the project-authored assembly only; no skeleton, skin weights or animation is included.
