# V9 armor — independent shoulder construction correction

Separate branch derived from frozen V8. Exclude every failed V9 cloth result; preserve that experiment and its unaccepted verdict separately. Preserve lean body proportions, mantle, top cap where possible, garment, cameras, sword and materials. No runtime rig, animation, AI or material work.

## Actual V8 diagnosis

Inspected `artifacts/art/last-vigil-v8/shoulder.png`. Each descending lame curls into an independent open arch with a deep mouth and long triangular corners. Exposed underlayer and black gaps interrupt the stack. This is authored surface construction, not a shader problem. Narrowing the whole stack or polishing edges cannot repair its overlapping relationship.

## Bounded replacement

Rebuild the three lower lames as strips derived from one shared tapered convex support surface following deltoid to upper arm. Retain V8's lean outer envelope and top-cap relationship. Each upper edge should hide approximately10–15mm under the preceding plate; exposed strip height initially30–40mm, adjusted to available actual span. These are feasible design trials, not historical measurements.

All cross-sections follow the same arm envelope. Avoid the upward curled mouth that turns each strip into a separate scoop. Front and rear ends return toward the arm and terminate in short rounded corners rather than long downward teeth. Use approximately2mm sheet thickness and a small continuous folded return; no decorative wire ring or separate ridge. Keep mounting and layer clearance plausible. Do not change the arm to fit a bad shell.

## Two-view shape gate

Render cheap actual clay shoulder three-quarter and side views. The descending outer silhouette should read as one articulated arc with overlapping strips. Reject open mouth gaps, exposed underlayer between strips, arm/mantle intersections, redundant double rims and widening beyond the lean V8 envelope. Plate boundaries should remain legible; do not fuse the stack into a smooth tube.

Only a visibly better actual surface relationship justifies retaining this branch. Two static views cannot prove articulation range, rig quality or collision clearance in motion. Overall artistic target remains unmet even if this local construction trial improves.

## First actual clay trial

Inspected both actual clay views. Lower strips project less than the original scoops, but direct fitting to stepped body surfaces creates jagged bands, and legacy rivets float off their replaced plates. Reject this state. The body is a clearance envelope, not a surface to emboss into armor. Agreed a smooth fitted support loft based on measured sections, with obsolete detached hardware removed.

The retained top cap still has a pronounced open mouth above the first new strip. Its hidden upper section must extend inside the cap while keeping the visible support arc smooth. Avoid exposed sleeve stripes and flatten the overly deep inverted-chevron boundary curves. No material changes can substitute for these construction fixes.

## Corrected actual review

Inspected the corrected clay shoulder, clay shoulder side and material hero. The shared smooth support surface now closes the large inter-lame mouths and removes the jagged body-sampling artifacts. Detached legacy hardware is no longer visible. The stack reads as overlapping armor rather than disconnected scoops; the lean V8 figure is retained, with its original garment rather than failed V9 cloth.

This is a meaningful local construction improvement worth preserving. The lowest rim still has a small open crescent, boundaries remain repetitive and the overall character retains its simplified smooth appearance. Do not turn this limited gain into reference-quality, articulation or global art approval. No further broad geometry loop is requested for this bounded branch absent a technical defect.
