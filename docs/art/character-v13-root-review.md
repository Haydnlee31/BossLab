# V13 actual visual review

## Initial shape and full-character baseline

Root visually inspected the approved concept target, V13 attempt-01 three-quarter clay and V10 front render. The concept is a design target, not built evidence.

V13 introduces a visible waist taper and less rounded breastplate. Initial shoulder bands still read as padded horizontal capsules; the cap apex is pointed accidentally, and the lower waist has a distracting horizontal swell. These issues were sent to the artist and director before continuation.

The approved target uses thin swept shoulder lames with shallow downward points, broad chest fields separated by curved transition ridges, and restrained fasteners. These construction differences matter more than surface noise. Artist is continuing real surface-seated chest framing, layered collar and full-character context.

V10 full-character baseline still has rounded joint cops, smooth cylindrical forearms, hard split-skirt folds, weak mail readability and broad plastic-like highlights. The V13 context review must assess those dominant forms rather than accepting a chest ornament as completion. No reference-quality acceptance has been granted.

## Construction and first context

Root inspected attempt-03 and attempt-04 three-quarter clay. The frame now follows the chest, the waist swell is reduced, shoulder lames have shallow descending boundaries, and three gorget layers replace the plain funnel. These are actual shape changes, still coarse.

Root inspected context-01 front. The new chest/shoulder section sits in the full figure and its worked steel is more convincing than the inherited clean chrome limbs. Missing studs in this context are an acknowledged append-transform defect under repair. The inherited padded collar reads as two coarse flaps; limbs and joints remain tubes/spheres, and the skirt remains rigid. Artist continues collar reconstruction, coordinated limb/joint shaping, consistent steel and textile construction. No overall reference-quality gate has passed.

## Context 02–03

Root inspected context-02 torso and context-03 front/side. Context02 fixes the missing studs and replaces the old collar flaps, but creates a broad horizontal shelf and continuous fringe. Artist ray diagnostics identify the pale inner trough as the collar itself, not an armor intersection. Context03 lowers the collar, improves forearm taper and brings inherited armor into a common steel response.

The new knee and elbow pieces read as rounded beans/knobs from the front; root, director and QA independently flagged this as a possible regression. They require flatter directional front fields and restrained asymmetric wings. Side view confirms the skirt and cape still read as rigid panels/straight wedge. These findings drive another coordinated shape pass and textile construction. No overall art gate has passed.

## Context 04–05

Root inspected context04 front, side and torso. Joint cop outlines are more directional than the prior rounded knobs. The skirt gains thigh volume and mail visibility, but its tops visibly cross the belt face, and the cape becomes inflated in side view. These are regressions requiring correction, not an accepted textile pass.

Root inspected context05 garment and side. Belt face is clear and the cape now falls toward the ankles with less inflation. The garment detail still shows isolated covered thigh buckles poking through cloth and sharp horizontal fitting ledges. Artist identified sparse obstacle-ray fitting and discontinuous cuisse constraints; a continuous conservative thigh envelope and corrected hidden hardware layer are next. Cape remains sparse and broad in its folds, without a reference-fidelity claim.

## Later full-character milestones and cape correction

Root inspected context06/07 hilt, context08 head/hand, context09 dorsal/palm, context11 hero, context12 back, context13 garment, context14/15/16 back and context16 side, plus the interim final front/clay-side. Actual leather grip wrapping and continuous swept guard ends replace the prior rods/buttons/coin-like terminals. Hand lames are real construction, but fingers/palm remain simplified. Crown shaping is restrained; no anatomy or historical-armor certification.

The context13 garment correction reduces the sharp mid-thigh shelf into a broader transition. Heavy smooth cloth and lower hem puckers remain. Intermediate final side/back exposed a detached-looking rectangular upper cape edge and a sharp pouch-side dogleg; root reopened the freeze because these were fixable construction defects. Context14 improved the side edge but introduced high top tabs. Context15 tucks the top beneath the collar but exposes pale cuirass through the return. Context16 adds a scoped upper-shell envelope correction: root independently sees the pale patch gone, top seated beneath collar and a smoother continuous side edge. This is a local static construction gain, not global contact or animation validation.

The model is more coherent than V10 across major armor, material, garment and sword features, but still falls well short of the approved concept's realism. Sparse broad cape folds, graphic embroidery, fringe-like fur, simple hand anatomy and generalized material finish remain. More procedural noise/ornament is not a credible substitute for bespoke sculpting, proper UVs and deliberate material painting. No reference-quality artistic acceptance.

Coherent final context16 evidence and saved readback completed behind the reserve guard. Independent QA confirms all11 views and26 delivery hashes. Overall artistic verdict remains needs work.
