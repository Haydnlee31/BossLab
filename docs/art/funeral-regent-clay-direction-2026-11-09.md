## Final candidate03 — rejected, correction cap reached

Directly inspected final `attempt-03/idle-front.png`, `idle-side.png`, `idle-back.png`, `fullcast-threequarter.png` and `reel-threequarter.png`. Handedness now matches the written contract, but this does not offset major regressions. Lower-arm volume is absent, hands appear detached, shoulder regions collapse in action poses, and dark intrusions interrupt the mask. The technical artist identifies failed open-shell union as a body-construction cause; the visible result independently fails regardless of that diagnosis. Long cloth remains disconnected board-like strips, with an open upper back instead of the selected shoulder-supported mourning mantle. The skulls are recognizable proxies, not authored skull anatomy.

**Reject the construction and pose gate.** No fourth correction, material expansion or game integration should follow this candidate. Preserve the source, actual failed images and diagnostic reports. The three-attempt exercise did not deliver a usable B foundation; numeric weights, saved-scene readback and distinct pose names cannot turn this into an accepted boss asset. Earlier local rib/hood changes do not constitute overall progress sufficient for production.

The next authoring method must begin with a verified watertight anatomical control mesh and actual shoulder/limb topology, then establish one clean deformed arm before adding the mantle. Build garment pattern surfaces around that verified body with shared attachment landmarks, rather than attaching four analytic strips or unioning open shells. Mask planes require deliberate face landmarks and fitted hood clearance. This recommendation is a future method change, not permission to bypass this study's correction cap or claim that Blender itself cannot achieve the design.
